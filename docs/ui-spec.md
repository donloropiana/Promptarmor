THE PRODUCT: lenders' compliance teams review marketing ads before they go out. Members connect their Google/Microsoft accounts once; ads people email or share with them arrive automatically, or anyone drops a file in. Within a minute each ad is checked against the law and the company's real rates; the problem words are quoted, the rule is named, and a fix is suggested. A person always approves one exact version; approving with open issues needs a written reason; if rates change, affected approved ads come back for review; every step is recorded. Demo workspace: fictional "ClearPath Financial". Inspiration: Linear, Stripe Radar's review queue, Vercel, Apple, Ramp: powerful but obvious.

STRUCTURE (exactly four places plus sign-in):
1. ADS (#ads, default route; the home).
   - EMPTY WORKSPACE: one large drop box ("Drop an ad to check it", also accepts pasted ad text, plus a "Choose file" button) and 3 sample chips to try instantly (e.g. a paid-social personal-loan ad, a credit-card email, and an affiliate PDF with hidden text aimed at the AI). Clicking a sample or dropping starts the check immediately (no preview step); product and channel are inferred and shown as editable chips; never block the check on them. Samples are flagged is_sample, excluded from every count, with a "Clear samples" action in Workspace settings.
   - WITH WORK: the Ads list with a slim drop bar on top ("Drop an ad here" + "New ad" button). Drop a file ANYWHERE in the window to add an ad (full-window drop overlay).
   - One quiet summary line + a metrics strip above the list: "7 need review · 2 revisions in · synced from 4 accounts 2 min ago" and the strip: minutes per ad, days to approval, approved first time, exceptions, AI spend today vs $10. Show "—" until there are 5 reviews (the seeded workspace has enough). Banners appear only when a connection breaks or AI spend passes 80% of the cap.
   - Tabs: Needs review (n) · Changes requested (n) · Approved (n) · All.
   - FOUR STATUSES ONLY: Needs review, Changes requested, Approved, Rejected. "Checking…" is a spinner, not a status. Small reason tags explain why an ad is where it is: New, V2 (a revision), Rates changed, Hidden text, With exceptions (on approved), Changed after approval, Is this an ad?. Red and orange are reserved for severity.
   - Sort: worst first (hidden text > critical > major > age).
   - ROW (one per ad, a "visual list", no grid, no masonry): 64px thumbnail (real image or PDF page 1; text-only ads like email/SMS/search show a neutral format icon), the ad's own headline, the top problem in quotes (e.g. “5.99% APR” — lowest real rate is 8.99%), a risk mark with an issue count, From (submitter or partner + source icon), status word, age. Keep it to these; no 12-column tables.
   - Unclear arrivals appear as rows tagged "Is this an ad?" with inline Yes / No buttons. Non-ads are skipped automatically behind a single line "14 skipped · show". Exact duplicates merge automatically ("Seen in 2 places"). There is NO separate sorting/triage tab or page.
   - Keys: J/K move, Enter opens, ? shows shortcuts.
   - The Approved tab is where approved ads are found: each approved row shows the exact version approved, who, when, With exceptions tag if any; opening it shows the approval record with the reviewer's measured active time and the AI cost.
2. AD PAGE (#ad-<id>).
   - Ad on the left (rendered creative: social/display drawn in CSS, email, landing page, PDF pages), issues on the right. Clicking an issue highlights the exact words; highlight any text in the ad -> "+ Add issue" (for things the AI missed).
   - Each issue: plain title, severity (Critical/Major/Minor, icon + text), quoted words, why it's a problem, the rule with its legal source link, a suggested fix with "Use this wording" (creates a new version and re-checks; never edits the current one; not for images/PDF layout: "Text suggestion only").
   - ONE per-issue action: "Not a problem" (key X) with a short reason. Every other open issue is on the fix list.
   - DECISIONS: "Approve V<n>" (key A), "Request changes" (key R), Reject lives in the overflow menu. If approving while issues are still open, the Approve sheet lists each open issue and asks for one written reason each (>= 40 chars) — that is the exception, recorded once — restates the exact version ("Approve V2 · 9f2c41"), and has an attestation checkbox. Approve is disabled until a hidden-text alert is acknowledged. Self-approval with exceptions is blocked when the workspace has more than one member. After any decision, the next ad opens automatically (auto-advance), with an undo toast.
   - Request changes: a note built automatically from the open issues (quote, rule, fix), editable, with "Copy as text"; saved in the app; status becomes Changes requested; the app never sends email. Then the page shows "Waiting for a revision. It appears here automatically when the submitter replies or updates the file," with a demo-labelled "Simulate revision from Gmail" button.
   - V2 OPENS ON THE SAME PAGE: a "What changed" view with an inline word diff and a summary "3 fixed · 1 still there · 1 new", each earlier issue labelled Fixed / Still there / New. The button reads "Approve V2".
   - HIDDEN TEXT (the demo's big moment): a pinned alert shows the hidden instruction as inert quoted text, states that the real issues were still flagged, and that the AI has no way to approve anything.
   - ASK ABOUT THIS AD: the ONLY AI chat in the product, a compact box in the right column on the ad page. It sees only this version, its issues and its rules; answers in plain text; no tools, no buttons in answers, no model-written links or images, no saved history (clears on leaving). Fixed note under it: "Reads and answers. Can't approve, reject or change rules." Canned answers in the mockup: why an issue was flagged, what a rule requires, a rewrite of a disclosure, and a polite refusal for "approve this" listing the open issues; on the hidden-text ad it explains the hidden text was treated as content.
   - History on the ad (who did what, checks and their cost, decisions) with "View as at decision". Product and channel are editable chips (audit event).
3. RULES (#rules default tab Rules, #rules-products for Products): one page, two tabs: "Rules" and "Products".
   - Rules: starter rules are already ON; each rule shows a plain title, what it checks, default severity, a link to its legal source (e.g. "Reg Z 12 CFR 1026.24(d)"); version history with redline vs previous; Edit with required reason creates a new version; turn a rule off/on.
   - Products: ClearPath's real terms (personal loan PL-1, rewards card CC-1, balance transfer card CC-2, mortgage prequal MG-1) with version history; Edit terms -> confirm "This sends N approved ads back to Needs review" -> affected approved ads move to Needs review tagged "Rates changed". Each check records which product version it used.
4. SETTINGS (via the workspace menu at the bottom of the sidebar; routes #members, #accounts, #workspace):
   - Members: members (owner/member), invite by email, pending invites with Send again / Revoke; can't remove the last owner.
   - Connected accounts: "Connect Google" (Gmail + Drive) and "Connect Microsoft" (Outlook + OneDrive), ONE click each; simulated consent (Google asks twice: Gmail then Drive; the provider is shown as "Nango"); then automatic discovery. Honest card copy BEFORE connecting: "Reads only. Looks back 14 days. Keeps only ads. Google will list wider permissions than we use; our code blocks those." Connections listed BY MEMBER (each member's accounts feed this workspace): e.g. "Hannah Ortiz · Gmail, Drive · synced 2 min ago", "Daniel Kim · Outlook · reconnect needed". Sync log drawer.
   - Workspace: name, AI daily cap, Clear samples, Export audit record (dialog with generated JSON + Copy; no downloads).
5. SIGN-IN (#signin, chromeless): email + "Email me a sign-in link" / "Email me a code" (6-digit entry; 482913 works) and a password tab with forgot password. NO Google/Microsoft sign-in buttons. Invite accept (#invite) and New workspace (#new-workspace) stay.
SIDEBAR NAV: Ads, Rules; workspace switcher at top; workspace menu at bottom (Members, Connected accounts, Workspace, Sign out). No Dashboard, Queue, Intake, Policies, Reports, Insights, Partners, campaigns, assignment, snooze, blind sampling, grid view, or global chat/Ask bar. Cmd/Ctrl+K is optional and, if present, only jumps to an ad or a rule (no questions).
NOTIFICATIONS: in-app only (bell list) for members, e.g. "Your ad A-105 was approved". The app sends no email except sign-in/invite emails (sent by the auth system). No reply-to, forwarding addresses, submitter portals or sending-domain settings anywhere.
WORDS: ad (not asset), workspace (not profile), issue (not finding), check (not preflight), Products, Connected accounts, From, Changes requested. UI text only (code names may stay).
KEEP (artifact contract): no <!DOCTYPE>/<html>/<head>/<body> in index.html; <title>Compliance Workbench</title>; viewport-fit meta + side gutter max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-*)); Geist fonts from Google Fonts only; no other external resources; no alert/confirm/prompt/print/window.open/downloads; hash routes of letters/digits/hyphens only (never '#/', '?', '=', or settings/sidebar/customize/upgrade/downloads/comment-*); localStorage only in try/catch; shadcn neutral tokens on :root with both dark blocks, no literal colors in components; status/severity distinguishable without color; one font family, 3 text sizes, tabular numbers, quoted ad text visually set apart; responsive to ~400px with no horizontal page scroll; visible focus; reduced motion; the prototype banner stays.
