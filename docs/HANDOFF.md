## The product (plain English)
A multi-tenant review desk for marketing that has to follow the law. Lenders (personal loans, credit cards, mortgages) must check every ad before it runs. Members of a workspace (e.g. the fictional demo company **ClearPath Financial**) connect their own Google/Microsoft accounts once; ads people email or share with them flow in automatically, or anyone drops a file/pastes copy. Within a minute the AI checks each ad against the law and the company's real product terms, quotes the exact problem words, names the rule, and suggests a fix. A human always approves one exact version; approving with open issues needs a written reason; if product terms change, affected approved ads go back for review; every step is recorded.

**UI source of truth: `docs/ui-spec.md`** (critique-approved structure: Ads list, ad page, Rules, Settings, sign-in).
